Echo Beat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Echo Beat ("the game") is developed by Teo Peeters. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information the game stores

Your level progress, stars, coins, unlocked ink sets and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers and do not collect this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional reward ads you choose to watch, and occasional ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google shares only an anonymous purchase token with the game so it can unlock what you bought.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
